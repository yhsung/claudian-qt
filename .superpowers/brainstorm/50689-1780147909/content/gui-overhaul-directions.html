A. Project-Scoped Capture Workspace

Open directly into capture, but always within a selected project. Sessions become part of a structured project work log.

Pros

  • Best match for project-first information architecture
  • Improves trust and navigation together
  • Lets directors and GMs consume the same knowledge later

Cons

  • Requires introducing project metadata and extracted record types
  • Needs stronger session-to-artifact linking than today

B. Global Capture Inbox

Optimize for speed of capture above all else, with organization delayed until after the fact.

Pros

  • Fastest possible ingestion path
  • Low friction for engineers dropping in raw material

Cons

  • Trust decays if sorting does not happen reliably
  • Navigation remains weak longer
  • Directors and GMs get value later, not sooner

C. Role Dashboard with Project Drill-Down

Design for leadership visibility from the start, with engineer capture as one part of a broader operational dashboard.

Pros

  • Strong long-term story for directors and GMs
  • Makes organizational summaries first-class

Cons

  • Too abstract for day-1 engineer adoption
  • High risk of becoming a superficial dashboard on weak data
What changes in all options

The current visible operator controls move behind an Advanced layer. The main surface becomes about capture, provenance, structure, and retrieval, not model knobs.
